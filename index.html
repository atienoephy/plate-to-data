The Problem

You're losing money and you can't point to why.

Every night

Food goes to waste

Overcooked batches, leftovers, spoiled stock. It happens daily and it happens quietly — nobody's stopping to add it up.

No record

No one tracks why

Was it over-prepped, or did it just not sell? Without a reason attached, the same mistake repeats next week.

Rush hour

Spreadsheets don't survive service

Nobody's opening Excel mid-rush. Whatever you use has to work in the ten seconds staff actually have.

How It Works

Three steps. Staff barely notice step one.

01

Log the waste

Dish, quantity, reason, date — a short form staff fill in from a phone or the kitchen tablet, right when it happens. No login friction, no extra steps during a rush.

02

Dashboard does the math

Every log rolls up automatically: total kg wasted this week, the top 3 wasted items, and the top reasons behind them. No manual tallying.

03

Act on the pattern

When the dashboard says rice is over-prepped every Monday, you cook less rice on Mondays. Guessing becomes a decision.
